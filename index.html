La Iguana Clips is the desktop publishing tool of the channel La Iguana | Clips GTA 6. It turns GTA 6 live streams into short vertical clips with subtitles and publishes them on YouTube Shorts and on TikTok.

La Iguana Clips es la herramienta de escritorio del canal La Iguana | Clips GTA 6: convierte directos de GTA 6 en clips verticales con subtítulos y los publica en YouTube Shorts y TikTok.

How it uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): the creator signs in with TikTok and authorizes the app. We read the basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID) to show which account the clips will be posted to.
  • Content Posting API – Direct Post (video.publish): once a clip is rendered, the app posts it to the authorized TikTok account with its caption, using the privacy options returned by TikTok for that creator.

The app only acts on the TikTok account that the user explicitly authorizes. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
