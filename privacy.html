This policy explains how La Iguana Clips ("the app", "we") handles information when you connect a TikTok account to it.

1. Information we access

When you authorize the app through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok gives us an access token and refresh token, and, with the user.info.basic scope, your basic profile: open ID, display name and avatar. With the video.publish scope we can post videos to your account and read the creator settings TikTok returns for posting (for example the allowed privacy levels).

We do not access your messages, followers list, contacts, payment data or any other TikTok data.

2. How we use it

  • To show which TikTok account is connected.
  • To upload and publish the short videos produced by the app to that account, with their captions.
  • To check the publishing status of each upload.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the computer where the app runs, in a local configuration file. They are sent only to TikTok's official API (open.tiktokapis.com) over HTTPS. We do not operate a server that stores your data.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); revoked tokens stop working immediately. You can also ask us to delete any locally stored data by contacting us.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country to use TikTok).

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
